## 3. How Enterprise Customers Use This at Scale

Applied to the two real topics engaged today — AI-assistant audit trails, and golden-signal alerting on payment/gateway services:

- **Audit trails:** Enterprises running AI assistants across large observability estates don't rely on chat replay for audit; they instrument the *outputs* of assistant-driven actions (dashboard/alert/SLO diffs, Git commit history) as the system of record. A Git-backed digest like this one is diffable and PR-reviewable even on days with zero activity, which itself is auditable evidence.
- **Golden-signal alerting at scale:** Large customers standardize a repeatable "Golden Signals" rule template (error rate, latency p95, traffic drop) per service and roll it out via folder-per-service organization, exactly as seen here across aci-gateway, aci-payments, aci-loadgen. This keeps alert semantics consistent across dozens or hundreds of services without bespoke thresholds per team.
- **SLO rule groups:** The `grafana_slo_*` recording-rule pattern (5m/30m/1h/2h/6h/1d/3d windows plus objective/info series) seen for aci-accounts, aci-fraud, and aci-ledger is how platform teams give every service team a consistent multi-window burn-rate SLO without each team hand-rolling PromQL.
- **Team structure:** Platform/SRE teams typically own the rule templates and folder structure; AppDev/service teams own the thresholds and notification routing within their folder — a federation model that scales alert ownership without centralizing every decision.
