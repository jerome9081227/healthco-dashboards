**SLO burn-rate alerting (order-service Latency, Checkout Success, Cart Performance, Ensemble):**
- Multi-window burn rate (5m through 3d) lets fast-burn page immediately while slow-burn just opens a ticket, which is the standard mechanism large orgs use to avoid alert fatigue while still catching gradual degradation.
