## Section 4 — Vertical Shifts

**Financial Services — the AI wedge inverts.** Citi, State Street, Cross River, Butter Payments, CardX, Alter Domus, Farmers all landed this week. In the two largest banks the AI story *created* the blocker: Grafana Assistant requires a Cloud uplink, corporate governance won't approve an external LLM, and both are now building on-prem + internal-LLM + custom-MCP paths. Meanwhile the *cost* story in FinServ is landing hard and quantitatively (Alter Domus 55%, Cross River $300K→$50–100K, Butter 4% of a committed tier). **Shift: lead FinServ with cost and governance, treat AI as a roadmap conversation, and lead with BYOC/on-prem before someone asks.**

**Regulated + sovereign — "AI is our differentiator" stops working.** ResetData (gov/defence, protected/secret), Citi, State Street: three independent calls where our AI headline was neutralized on deployment-model grounds, not capability grounds. New required muscle: name the Cloud-uplink requirement early and pivot to Enterprise + custom MCP + internal LLM before the customer discovers it.

**Healthcare — Grafana is being scoped against BI tools, not just APM.** Guardant Health named **New Relic and Tableau** as displacement targets in the same breath, and the use case was business-process reconciliation (Mule↔LIMS order flow) rather than infra. Oscar Health quantified self-managed maintenance at $1.2–1.4M/yr. Florida Blue went the other way — already signed Datadog, migrating off Splunk, and reports Datadog *cheaper* than Splunk with no cost spike yet. **Shift: healthcare is buying data consolidation and business-process visibility; and Datadog is winning Splunk displacements in healthcare where we aren't in the room.**

**Retail / Restaurant — the buying trigger is edge and peak season, and the buyer isn't the platform team.** Inspire Brands: Orane owns in-store/restaurant tech (network, firewall, switches, registers, kitchen displays), wants to go **20% → 80% proactive**, is a Meraki shop — but Lakshmi's cloud/mobile/Splunk-focused infrastructure team holds approval and his edge requirements get overlooked. TechStyle: October deadline set by Black Friday. Abercrombie: unspent K6 funds are the entry point, cost + vendor lock-in on Splunk/Dynatrace is the driver. **Shift: in retail, sell to the edge/store-ops persona and let peak season set the clock — but map the platform-team approver on call one.**

**Manufacturing / Industrial / OT — a genuinely new segment showing up inbound.** Jabil (130 locations, chargeback per site, cert-expiry catches preventing factory shutdown), Sibanye Stillwater (gold plant thickener flow rates, 8kg gold per incident), Custom Fleet (Azure migration, 1-month timeline, no internal implementation capacity). The economics here are physical and enormous, the observability maturity is low, and **two of three came inbound.** Common need: implementation delivered *for* them (partner or Grafana), because they have no capacity.

**AI-native / platform teams — a new buying trigger: "we're shipping AI-written code."** PlayHQ (AI DLC pipeline, observability-as-QA in place of code review, one referenced customer at 60% automated development), Landsort (inconsistent observability makes the AI push "extra scary"), Ford (synthetic pre-prod telemetry). The question shifted from *how do I monitor my LLM app* to *how do I trust code my agents wrote*. Cost attribution for AI-generated telemetry came up unprompted.

**APAC — POC mechanics are the deal risk.** CardX/IZENO (late into a Dynatrace-shaped RFP, POC timing renegotiated), Custom Fleet (skip POC, go straight to pilot, expect Grafana to share risk, no AU Azure region), 2P Perfect Presentation (400 projects, POC at Taiba University, resource assigned while the SE is on PTO to mid-Sept). **Shift: in APAC the loss mode this quarter is resourcing and scheduling, not product.**

## Signal of the Week

Two things moved. **First: our AI wedge inverted in regulated accounts** — Citi, State Street and ResetData all neutralized Grafana Assistant on deployment model (Cloud uplink, external LLM governance, sovereign/protected data), not on capability, so in FinServ and gov/defence we should lead with cost, governance and BYOC/on-prem and treat AI as roadmap. **Second: the Datadog wedge sharpened from "cheaper" to "you're paying for data nobody queries"** — Butter Payments used 4% of a $6K/mo committed log tier and Philadelphia Inquirer estimates 25–30% of telemetry unused, while Flipdish and Butter both confirm the retention mechanism is UI familiarity, not features. Underneath both: a new, high-value inbound segment (manufacturing and mining — Jabil preventing factory shutdowns, Sibanye at 8kg of gold per incident) where the economics are physical, the maturity is low, and the blocker is that they have no capacity to implement it themselves.
